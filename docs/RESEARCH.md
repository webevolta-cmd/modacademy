## 8. Design research (summary)

About 30 education sites were reviewed (CG Spectrum, NABA, Domus Academy, Shillington, SPD, Learn Squared, Domestika, ECAL, SVA, KABK, among others). Patterns adopted: audience-labelled course rows with key facts inline; a small filter set; a comparison table; a short “find your course” helper (CG Spectrum/NABA); course pages that open with a key-facts block; separating inquiry from enrollment; a showroom-learning strip (Domus-style company workshops); restrained, editorial typography and scroll motion. Anti-patterns avoided: fake counters, stock portraits, unexplained prices, hover-only information and long forced scroll intros.
